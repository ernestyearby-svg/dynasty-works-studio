## Intake and organization

Keep approved masters in a controlled source archive. Store only approved web derivatives under public/assets/projects/{mymosa,ikla-maison,smokesuite,mr-cliffs,ohana-to-alpine}/. Do not place unknown-status assets in public. Each media record supports dimensions, descriptive alt text, optional caption and explicit srcSet. Preserve original files separately. Record approval against the exact case revision including copy, all media, rights and credits before setting publicationApproval and published status. Revoke approval before revising that approved record.

Archive entries have their own approval reference and are filtered before being sent to the browser. /creative-review is a non-navigation, noindex route inside the owner-private site; it is not a separate authentication boundary. Its asset register lists requested areas, not delivered work.
