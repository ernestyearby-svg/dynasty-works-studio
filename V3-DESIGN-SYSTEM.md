Directly reviewed https://www.pentagram.com/, https://www.akqa.com/ and https://www.rga.com/ plus R/GA's Android case study. Observations are design interpretations, not claims about conversion performance.

## Original thesis
An open editorial index: monumental declarations, authentic product interruptions, offset image/text chapters and a continuous thin Workline. The line measures the declaration, marks a chapter and indicates a Foundry step. The Foundry is an interactive transformation of an idea into a company, not a factory image.

## Tokens
Base: warm paper #F4F1EA / soft white #FAF9F6 / carbon #171717 / graphite #1F1F1F. Rules use currentColor at restrained opacity; silver #B8B8B2 used only for neutral rules. Project colors remain local to their exhibit; MyMosa orange is a surrounding exhibition field, never a packaging recolor.
Type: existing licensed self-hosted Bodoni Moda (declaration/editorial) and Manrope (information/system). Display 48–208px, editorial titles 40–112px, body 16–22px, secondary metadata minimum12px, controls minimum14px. Fluid scales, explicit mobile treatment.
Grid: open 12-column idea expressed through 1:2 / 2:1 editorial proportions. Gutters 24–64px; section space 72–160px. Thin rules; no default rounded cards.
Breakpoints: 700px mobile, 1000px tablet, existing1200px navigation transition. Test320–2560px. Images contain authentic product masters; no accidental product cropping.
Motion: one grammar—line growth and brief image scale on deliberate hover/focus. Foundry changes on explicit selection, never auto-plays. Existing reduced-motion preference overrides all motion. Content visible without animation.
