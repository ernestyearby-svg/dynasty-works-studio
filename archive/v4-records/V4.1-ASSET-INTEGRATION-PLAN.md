## Composition

Keep the light hero and evolve its existing assembly into point, line, boundary and restrained material resolution. No photograph behind the headline. Raw Idea introduces the material world afterward.

Use one active discipline visual at a time. Strategy and Operations remain browser-native. Add an explicit Packaging control under Brand. Preserve keyboard tab behavior and touch access.

Keep the four methodology phases but use a single controlled stage: native Define, Material Craft for Build, Physical Experience for Launch and Scale for expansion. Material Craft therefore gets a deliberate moment without an extra full-width image section.

Place Human Creation once, as a compact editorial bridge. Do not identify the generated person as a team member. Keep the real Builder prominent and functional; avoid repeating the Digital asset as a large device mockup.

Keep capability accordions light, with selected contextual fragments only when expanded. Add five text-led work territories without client imagery. End with Future Experiment, then a light What are we building? conversion section.

## Performance and accessibility

After inspecting sources, create AVIF and WebP derivatives with intrinsic dimensions, srcset and sizes. Preserve masters separately. Load only active interactive imagery; lazy-load below-fold scenes. Choose mobile positions from actual subjects and typography. No new image generation.

Use conceptual descriptions, visible classification where imagery could imply real people or locations, and no claims based on environmental lettering. Motion must respond to both the OS preference and V4's existing Reduce motion control. No scroll hijacking or autoplay loops.
