## Interface and narrative

Hero keeps From idea to company and cobalt emphasis. Its existing interaction now resolves a point into a boundary and material-toned geometry without a hero photograph.

What We Build preserves six disciplines and keyboard navigation. Strategy and Operations use native system studies; other selections mount one visual at a time. Methodology preserves Define / Build / Launch / Scale content in four controls and one visual stage.

Company Builder remains the live primary tool. Digital imagery appears in capability discovery with a direct connection to the tool, avoiding another large device image beside it. The capability range preserves native disclosures and only mounts contextual imagery when selected. The work gateway adds Consumer, Fashion, Product, Hospitality and Technology as typographic territories; no client brands or thumbnails appear.

The final conversion returns to paper: What are we building? Start with the idea. We'll help map what comes next. Start a company and Talk to the studio remain real destinations.

## Mobile art direction

Desktop uses wide editorial frames. Mobile uses square subject crops, with right-focused positioning recorded in the registry to remove partial left-side environmental headings and keep product/person subjects substantial. No new mobile image generation. Context and classification remain accessible HTML outside images.

## Motion / accessibility

Controlled aperture and selection reveals; user-triggered hero assembly. No scroll hijacking, autoplay loop, animation dependency or decorative parallax. OS reduced-motion rules and the existing manual control disable transitions. Meaningful images have conceptual alt text. People and environments are not presented as documentary evidence. Original embedded typography is retained as supplied, not used as accessible content.

Keyboard discipline navigation, Packaging buttons, methodology buttons and accordion activation verified. Manual reduced motion returns 0s transition and no aperture clipping. Native controls retain V4 focus outlines and labels. Palette unchanged: cobalt/white 6.03:1 and graphite/white 6.83:1. OS preference handling reviewed in CSS; no OS emulation claim.
