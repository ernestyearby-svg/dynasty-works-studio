# Service catalog — V1.3

## Taxonomy and ownership

`data/service-catalog.ts` is the authoritative structured catalog: 122 services across Start (14), Brand (18), Build (15), Launch (15), Distribute (17), Activate (16), Grow (13), Publish (14). IDs are practice-prefixed stable slugs, so Launch Content Systems and Publish Content Systems remain distinct. `data/services.ts` preserves the twelve legacy editorial disciplines for compatibility; it is not a second commercial engine. Practice summaries show a small catalog selection, while /capabilities progressively discloses all entries.

Each service includes ID, slug, name, practice, descriptions, buying modes, business types/stages, prerequisites, next services, proposed deliverables, relative complexity, delivery type, professional boundary, featured/active flags and roadmap phase. Proposed service deliverables are scope discussion prompts, not contractual promises. Business-type lists describe applicability, not eligibility for licensed activity. Runtime physical-market gates further constrain distribution and activation.

## Professional boundaries

DIRECT covers creative, research, strategy and technology execution. COORDINATED covers process guidance, administrative coordination and market programs requiring external permissions. PROFESSIONAL_REQUIRED is a reusable classification for regulated work that the studio must refer out. No regulated professional service is offered as a studio catalog item. The roadmap groups studio work, coordination and possible specialist requirements in plain language. Legal/tax advice, regulated filings, permission decisions and regulated distribution require authorized outside professionals. No provider relationship or authorization is implied.

## Publication

/capabilities and eight /capabilities/[practice] routes provide practice descriptions, audiences, service disclosures, work evidence gates and next steps. /services remains compatible. Individual /services/[slug] pages are deliberately absent until substantial approved content exists. Related case studies only render when published; existing placeholder portfolio content remains clearly identified.

## Review queue

Approve service-specific scope, deliverable depth, capacity, exclusions, professional jurisdictions and portfolio evidence before a public commercial launch. No rate cards or internal margins belong in this catalog.
