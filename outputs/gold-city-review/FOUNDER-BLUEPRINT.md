# Founder Blueprint — canonical approved engagement

## Offer

**Founder Blueprint · $1,500 USD.** This is the only engagement currently approved for public pricing. Strategic company development: turn the idea into a buildable company roadmap. The canonical public data is `data/founder-blueprint.ts`; the package engine references its approved price. Other packages remain unpriced.

For founders with ideas, early businesses, founders preparing to launch, concepts needing structure and businesses organizing remaining company architecture. A mature company with a defined execution need does not automatically need this engagement.

## Approved deliverables

1. 60–90 minute strategy session.
2. Concept assessment: business concept, opportunity, business-model considerations and current stage.
3. Initial market and competitor research; positioning considerations.
4. Company foundation roadmap: entity process, administrative requirements, applicable licensing/permit research, trademark process and professional resources.
5. Brand roadmap: naming assessment where needed, brand strategy, identity, packaging where applicable and creative requirements.
6. Digital roadmap: website, relevant store/application, automation and customer journey.
7. Commercialization roadmap: sales materials, relevant distribution/retail readiness and buyer materials.
8. Launch roadmap: launch, content, campaign and activation considerations.
9. Prioritized 30/60/90-day execution sequence.
10. Professionally designed Dynasty Works Founder Blueprint digital document/PDF, reviewed before delivery.

The offer does not include execution of branding, websites, regulated filings or distribution; these are separate engagements. Legal/tax advice and regulated services must be performed by qualified outside professionals. Third-party professional fees are separate. A 30/60/90 plan is a planning sequence, not a promised completion schedule.

## Current routes and conversion

/founder-blueprint shows the approved scope and price. /founder-blueprint/intake provides five progressive screens with validation, editable review, clear and local text download. No submission, payment, upload, booking or automatic email occurs. The public offer exists within the existing privately hosted review Site.

Builder recommendations explain the fit from stage, assets and scope. Early founders with little infrastructure, multiple interdependent needs or uncertainty may receive Blueprint. DIY preference is preserved. Mature single-service work stays with the appropriate direct or growth engagement. A recommendation is not an accepted purchase.

## Future document architecture

The 21-section structure in `blueprintDocumentSections` covers Cover, Executive Snapshot, The Idea, Current Position, Opportunity, Business Model, Market Landscape, Company Foundation, Brand Architecture, Product / Service Requirements, Digital Infrastructure, Commercialization, Launch, Distribution / Retail (conditional), Activation (conditional), Professional Resources Required, 30-Day Roadmap, 60-Day Roadmap, 90-Day Roadmap, Recommended Dynasty Works Engagement and Next Actions.

No research or client-specific document content is seeded. Intake → sourced research → structured internal draft → human review → revisions → approved PDF → authorized private client vault. `server/blueprint-document.ts` requires approval of the exact revision, reviewer, timestamp and private object path before delivery eligibility. Changing a revision invalidates prior approval. Actual rendering, storage and delivery are not implemented.
