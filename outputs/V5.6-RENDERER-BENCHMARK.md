## Technique
Direct Three.js WebGL2, shaded box geometry mapped from retained SVG coordinates, fixed architectural camera, MeshStandardMaterial, PCF shadow map, warm directional and aperture lighting, distance fog, low-resolution local cube probe for rough reflection. No post-processing, bloom, orbit controls, camera motion or continuous idle render loop. Rendering occurs at initialization/resize/visibility restore; a bounded 120-frame sample runs only when the QA event is explicitly dispatched. This is approximate PBR lighting, not ray tracing or global illumination. Lighting and reflections remain a prototype requiring visual judgment.
